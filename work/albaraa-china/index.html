04 — Vlog Edit

Al-Baraa — China Vlog

Client
Al-Baraa
Year
2025
Role
Edit · Sound · Colour
Kit
Premiere Pro · After Effects

A travel vlog cut down from hours of handheld footage into a story with a shape — arrival, discovery, and a place that stays with you.

The problem

Vlog rushes have no script to fall back on. The structure has to be found in the edit.

The approach

Sequences are grouped by feeling before they are grouped by chronology, and the sound of the place is left running underneath so the cuts never feel like a slideshow.
