01 — Product Reel

Mastak

Client
Mastak
Year
2026
Role
Direction · Cinematography · Edit · Colour
Kit
Sony A7 III · Sony 50mm f/1.8 · Premiere Pro

A product reel built for the scroll. Shot entirely on one 50mm prime so the frame stays close to the product and never drifts into wide, generic coverage.

The problem

A viewer decides in under a second. The opening frame has to hold before the brand has said anything at all.

The approach

Motion is kept inside the frame instead of in the camera — the product moves, the light moves, the lens stays honest. Cuts land on the beat so the reel keeps pulling forward.
